Understand the idea

`LISTEN` and `NOTIFY` provide lightweight notifications between sessions. They are useful as wake-up signals, but notifications are not a durable message queue: consumers can be disconnected and payloads are limited. Persist business events when loss is unacceptable. Start with the result the application needs or the rule the database must protect. Choose a PostgreSQL feature that expresses that requirement, then identify evidence that would show it is correct.

Worked example

LISTEN order_events;
-- In another session: NOTIFY order_events, '{"order_id":42}';

Notifications are transient signals to connected listeners, not durable queue records. Persist events when consumers must recover after disconnects.
